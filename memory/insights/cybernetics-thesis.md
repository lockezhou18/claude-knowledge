**Thesis:** Agentic systems should be designed cybernetically: use harnesses, memory, reflection, and evaluation to stabilize probabilistic behavior under uncertainty; then compile successful patterns into reusable software. AutoResearch is one early example of this principle in action.

**Why:** 10 research agents, 338+ academic sources, ~850K characters of analysis (2026-04-15) confirmed: zero papers apply classical cybernetics directly to LLM agent design, yet the field independently rediscovered Ashby's ultrastability, Beer's VSM, and Tsien's meta-synthesis. The convergence validates this as the native theoretical language for agentic systems.

**How to apply:**
- AgentBus IS this principle applied to multi-agent coordination — the protocol is the deterministic shell, the agents are the probabilistic core, and the development process is the cooling schedule
- Every design decision traces to a theorem (Ashby's variety, Conant-Ashby's Good Regulator, Beer's S1-S5, Tsien's meta-synthesis)
- Features crystallize from repeated use, not from roadmaps — observe what works, harden it
- The harness grows; the core stays probabilistic (Principle 10)
