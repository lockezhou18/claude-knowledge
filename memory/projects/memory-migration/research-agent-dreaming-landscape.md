Surveyed 25+ agent memory/dreaming systems. Key findings:

**OpenClaw** has 3-phase biological sleep model (Light/REM/Deep) with 6-signal scoring and promotion thresholds (minRecallCount 3 + minScore 0.8) — convergent with our dual-threshold graduation.

**Pluton** (Hermes Agent plugin) has 6 analytical perspectives for knowledge extraction and organic wiki with wikilinks — richer than our 2-track categorization.

**ByteRover** (arXiv:2604.01599) is closest overall architecture — file-based markdown, maturity tiers, importance scoring, recency decay.

**No published system combines all 5 of our mechanisms:** scoring, graduation, pruning, staleness detection, and session-end retrospectives.

**High-value additions identified:**
1. Richer extraction perspectives (from Pluton) — low effort
2. Wikilinks between insights (from Pluton/GAAMA) — medium effort
3. Ebbinghaus decay curves (from SuperLocalMemory) — low effort
4. Two-phase consolidation NREM+REM (from TraceMem) — medium effort
